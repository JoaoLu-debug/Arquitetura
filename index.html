WE DELIVER UNCOMPROMISING ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTIONS GUIDED BY SWISS FUNCTIONALISM, RIGID STRUCTURAL GRID PATTERNS, AND THE HONEST UTILITY OF RAW CONCRETE.

THIS IS THE ERGO HEADQUARTERS IN THE HEART OF OPORTO: ONE OF THE MOST AMBITIOUS URBAN ARCHITECTURAL INITIATIVES IN SOUTHERN EUROPE, METICULOUSLY ARCHITECTED TO INTEGRATE STRUCTURE AND LANDSCAPE.

The building's structures balance structural gravity with a system of exposed load-bearing concrete cores. The Oporto studio is committed to designing spaces that foster collective interaction.
